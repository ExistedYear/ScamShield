The problem it solves

Why SMS phishing is hard to catch

  • Messages are tiny — often under 160 characters of context.
  • Attackers rewrite wording constantly to slip past filters.
  • Legitimate bank OTPs and recharge alerts look like spam.
  • Hundreds of millions of users still get fooled in multiple languages.

How ScamShield handles it

  • Multilingual encoder trained on 100 languages, fine-tuned on 30k+ messages.
  • Decision threshold raised to 0.55 to stop flagging Indian bank SMS.
  • Synthetic legitimate transactional SMS added to correct class imbalance.
  • Every prediction is attributable — word-level, not a bare score.

What the Android app adds
  • Real inbox monitoring — polls the Android SMS store every 15s and scans new messages automatically.
  • Background scanning — keeps checking when the app is closed and raises a local notification on a threat.
  • Encrypted channel — every message is encrypted with AES-256-CBC before it leaves the device.
  • Risk inbox — colour-coded badges across the whole conversation list.

Built for

ScamShield is for

  • Anyone receiving bank OTPs, prize notices or "verify your account" texts.
  • Caregivers helping less technical family members stay safe.
  • Researchers studying multilingual social-engineering patterns.

Not a replacement for

  • Judging intent — it scores patterns, it does not read intent.
  • Verified sender identity — check the number, not just the text.
  • Reporting — forward suspicious messages to your bank and the authorities.
